About Us

Welcome to Chitkara University's Tagore Library, a cornerstone of academic excellence and research support. We provide a diverse collection of resources, including books, journals, and digital materials across various disciplines. Our comfortable reading rooms and collaborative study spaces foster an ideal learning environment. With modern computer facilities and high-speed internet access, users can easily engage with online resources. Our expert librarians offer personalized assistance and workshops to enhance research skills. To ensure a conducive atmosphere, we have established library rules, including maintaining silence and respecting library property. The library operates from 9:00 am to 9:00 pm- Monday to Saturday and 9:00 amto 4:30 pm on Sundays.

Note: Food and drinks are not permitted, and personal belongings should be minimal. We are committed to supporting your academic journey and helping you achieve your educational goals.
